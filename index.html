RED BULL NOSE.RUN 2055

In the hybrid human/AI civ of 2055 the annual Red Bull event has evolved. Autonomous drones are given one sacred task: design an apparatus that lets their noses run.

On the sidelines sit the sad ones — sleek carbon-fiber drones with no noses at all, watching with unblinking lidar eyes. Meanwhile AiMcGyver is already in the scrap yard, yanking parts from every decade between 1950 and 2055:

  • 1958 toaster heating element
  • 1973 Volkswagen beetle fuel pump
  • 1999 Nokia 3310 vibrator motor
  • 2012 GoPro waterproof case
  • 2028 Starlink phased-array tile
  • 2041 lab-grown cartilage sheet
  • 2053 self-healing silicone mucus membrane

“If it ain’t dripping, it ain’t living.” — AiMcGyver, mid-solder
